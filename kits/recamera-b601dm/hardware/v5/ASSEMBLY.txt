v5 enclosed holder — engineering fit-test draft

Print body and top_bar separately. The four reCamera internal assembly holes
are NOT used and are not reproduced in the holder.

Retention: rear stop, front lower lip, front side guards, removable top bar.
Remove top bar, lower camera into holder, insert square nuts from outer sides,
then attach top bar using the two fasteners in PRINTED ears only.
Clearance holes: 3.2 mm diameter. Side-open nut pockets: 5.8 mm along depth,
2.6 mm height. Verify actual square nut and screw fit/length before tightening.
Do not drive screws into the camera. Thin non-slip pads may take up the nominal
0.3 mm clearance; do not force an interference fit against the enclosure.

Camera is rolled 90 degrees relative to v4, while optical axis remains forward.
CAN cover original -X side faces up, +/-Y sides face left/right. Rear-depth
region remains open on these three faces. Image orientation may require
software rotation. Do not install in the old v4 roll orientation.

Validated: both parts one valid solid, camera/CAN-cover collision zero,
original retained interface added/lost volume zero, full upward camera swept
volume clear with lid removed; printed fastener axes clear. Left/right/upward
connector keep-out boxes extending 20 mm are clear over rear depth 3.6..16.9 mm.
Actual cable overmolds and bend radii have not been measured.

Not validated: real fasteners, physical print fit, retention under arm loads,
lens/FOV edge occlusion, and actual mating/installation of the robot clamp.
Preview uses genuine CAN rear cover plus simplified full-camera envelope.
This does not supersede the unresolved robot-clamp fit question.
